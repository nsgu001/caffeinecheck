Trimester Notes

First Trimester

Most sensitive period. Caffeine crosses placenta easily. Consider staying under 150mg if possible.

Second Trimester

Standard 200mg limit applies. CYP1A2 enzyme activity decreases, so caffeine stays longer.

Third Trimester

Caffeine half-life triples. Consider reducing to 150mg. Avoid caffeine after 2pm.

How It Works

🔬 Calculation Principle

The tool tracks your daily caffeine budget against the 200mg NHS/FDA pregnancy limit. Each trimester has different metabolic considerations: first trimester is most sensitive, third trimester shows tripled half-life. The ring chart visualizes your remaining budget in real time.

📝 How to Use

Select your trimester, choose a beverage from the database or enter a custom amount, then click "Add to Budget." The ring chart updates instantly. Use the Trimester Notes to understand why limits vary by pregnancy stage.

💡 Pro Tips

• First trimester: consider staying under 150mg.
• Third trimester: half-life triples — avoid caffeine after 2 PM.
• Decaf still has 5mg per cup — it counts.
• Export your budget log for your prenatal appointments.
