How It Works

🔬 Calculation Principle

The estimator calculates your personalized caffeine cutoff time based on your bedtime, sensitivity level, and last dose. It uses a 5-hour log₂ clearance model: hours to clear = 5 × log₂(dose/threshold), where threshold is 50mg (high), 100mg (medium), or 200mg (low) sensitivity.

📝 How to Use

Set your target bedtime, select your sleep sensitivity (High if even 50mg disrupts you), and enter your last caffeine dose. Click Calculate to see your recommended cutoff time and predicted sleep impact.

💡 Pro Tips

• High sensitivity = cutoff 6-8 hours before bed.
• Medium sensitivity = cutoff 4-5 hours before bed.
• Low sensitivity = cutoff 2-3 hours before bed.
• Save estimates to track how sensitivity changes over time.
