How It Works

🔬 Calculation Principle

The taper schedule uses linear dose reduction: (current - target) / days = daily reduction. Symptom severity is estimated from caffeine dose: >500mg = Severe, >300mg = Moderate, >150mg = Mild. Onset, peak, and duration are derived from clinical withdrawal literature.

📝 How to Use

Enter your current daily dose, target dose, and preferred taper duration (7-30 days). Select a start date. The generator produces a day-by-day schedule with target doses and reduction steps. The dashboard shows expected symptom onset, peak severity, and total duration.

💡 Pro Tips

• Slower tapers (21-30 days) = milder withdrawal.
• Fast tapers (7 days) = intense but brief symptoms.
• Headaches peak at 20-51 hours after last dose.
• Export your schedule to stay accountable.
