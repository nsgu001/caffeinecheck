How It Works

🔬 Calculation Principle

Caffeine clearance follows first-order kinetics: the amount remaining halves every 2.5 hours (fast), 5 hours (average), or 9 hours (slow). The formula is C(t) = C₀ × 0.5^(t/t½), where t½ is your metabolizer-type half-life.

📝 How to Use

Enter your dose, consumption time, and select your CYP1A2 metabolizer status. The tracker plots a 24-hour clearance curve and shows when you drop below 50mg and 25mg thresholds — critical for sleep planning.

💡 Pro Tips

• Fast metabolizers: half-life ~2.5h. Slow: ~9h.
• If you don't know your type, start with Average.
• The 50mg threshold is when sleep becomes easier.
• Compare Fast vs Slow side-by-side to see the genetic gap.
